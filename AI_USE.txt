## AI use

**AI assistance used:** Claude (Anthropic) was used to draft the parser,
AST nodes, grammar, tests, demo script, and this README, based on the
assignment text and the group's lexer.

## AI Suggestion we verified:
Our group used AI during this project to help us understand how to
build the parser, especially recursive-descent parsing, operator precedence,
AST nodes, and syntax error handling We also used it to review some of our code,
tests, grammar, and README. One suggestion from AI was to separate the parser
into functions for expressions, terms, unary expressions, and primary expressions.
We did not use the suggestion exactly as it was given. We changed parts of it to fit
our lexer and AST design, and then tested it with different examples. For example, we
tested 2 + 3 * 4 to make sure multiplication was handled before addition and that 
the AST was correct. We also tested variables, assignments, print statements,
parentheses, multiple statements, and invalid syntax. Our parser passed all 22 tests.
Using AI helped us understand the parser better, but we learned that we still need to
check and test AI suggestions ourselves because not every suggestion will work correctly
with our project.
